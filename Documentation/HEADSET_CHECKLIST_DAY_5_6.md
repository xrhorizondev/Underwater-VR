# Combined Day 5–6 headset review

Use Builds/BlenderSquid/quest.apk after the approved squid replacement. This includes Day 5, Day 6, fish-light, distant-fish geometry and unused Android-depth corrections. The prior depth-fix build was wearer-confirmed smoother with a 72–73 FPS sampled result; this new squid build still needs its own appearance/performance check. Mark each item Pass / Issue / Not tested. For an issue, record the habitat, creature, action and a short headset recording if convenient.

## Controls and setup

1. Cold-launch the app. Check loading delay, correct head/hand tracking, breathing and visible lower-peripheral bubbles. Turn left/right and fully around; note freezes, judder or late-loading geometry.
2. Swim with the left stick and paddle with the grip gesture. Look around while moving. Check movement feels comfortable and does not pull you into rocks.
3. Open the habitat menu with the left menu button. Use the right stick to select, A to travel and Y/left menu to resume. Confirm all eight habitat destinations work. The right system button should retain its normal Quest function.
4. In the menu, use B to turn creature facts/narration off and on. With facts off, safe encounters should still react physically, without fact cards/narration. Hazard cautions must still work.
5. Gaze at a clam shell and press A to teleport. Check the three shells in each habitat, clear landings and no old bubble trail at the destination.

## Earlier habitats — regression checks

6. Sunlit Reef: approach clownfish, watch them scatter/reform, and scoop a selected small fish with right grip. It should sit near the palm, wriggle and release after about two seconds. Releasing and pressing grip should allow another scoop.
7. Check fish and both turtles from both sides: visible eyes, no buried pupils, broken materials or detached parts. Inspect the turtle's head/blink and the manta's motion. Report whether their responses feel convincing; the brief's full manta barrel roll and hand-directed turtle detail remain final-polish audit items.
8. Fire coral/lionfish: reach slowly. Expect soft red proximity feedback, then an icon/short warning, then brief haptics/red screen edges if you touch after the warning. Back away and retry; warnings should reset without continuously firing.
9. Rocky Ledge: check the anemone retracts/reopens, octopus changes colour/escapes/recovers, and lionfish keeps moving. The exact resting-octopus wake/eye/sucker choreography remains a final-polish audit item.
10. The Wreck: moray opens its mouth and retreats safely, grouper remains calm, and three dolphins loop without crowding or entering the wreck. Swim through the stern passage into darker water. Check optional facts are readable and short.

## Day 5 — Twilight Drift and The Trench

11. Enter Twilight Drift. Lumen should gradually become brighter, with no lure or point light attached. Approach and look around: following should stay smooth rather than snapping with head turns.
12. Continue through later habitats. Lumen should loosely follow, keep clear of rocks and stay reasonably easy to find. After menu travel, allow it time to catch up; it does not teleport beside you.
13. Reach toward comb jellies. They should drift gently away and remain harmless: no red warning, haptics or contact punishment. Bell and tentacles should remain attached. Their optional facts are text-only.
14. Descend into The Trench. Check continuous darkening and fog with enough blue/terrain/particle reference to navigate. Look for obvious lighting pops, pure-black areas, distracting marine snow or disorienting surface appearance.
15. Find two anglerfish. Reach close to one: only that fish should brighten. Leave it alone for several seconds: it should dim again. Keeping a hand there should not retrigger repeatedly; moving away and reaching again should work. Check swimming, lure and eyes remain intact.
16. Watch the trench squid from a distance. Its complete body/tentacles should move and stay clear of terrain. It is an observation encounter, with no touch reward or damage.

## Day 6 — Jellyfish Drift, Open Water Drift, Wreck & Rays

17. Jellyfish Drift: compare moon and box jellies. Moon jelly provides proximity/caution only, with no haptics/red-edge consequence. Box jelly retains all three stages. Watch for detached tentacles or warnings from the wrong surface.
18. Open Water Drift: find the recurring turtle near the central route. Check calm swimming, head/blink, visible eyes and the optional short fact. It should not become dangerous or grabbable.
19. Find the reef shark off to the side. Watch while stationary and approach slowly: no caution. Swim quickly toward it for roughly a second, within about 14 m: expect “Let's not chase it, just watch.” It should keep its distance and never produce contact punishment.
20. Continue watching/pursuing briefly: the shark cue should not repeat rapidly. Its cooldown is 18 seconds. Teleporting into the habitat should not itself count as pursuit. Check the new procedural shark's silhouette, fins and motion in-headset.
21. Look farther out for the recurring passing squid. It should remain a distant spectacle, move continuously and clear the reef. Gaze at it to reveal its optional fact; the card should appear within reading range. Check the scale and distance feel appropriate.
22. Wreck & Rays: gently reach toward a ray's body or wing. Expect a modest increase in wing motion, fading back afterward, with no warning/damage from the body. Reaching toward the tail/barb should retain the separate red outline, caution and warned-contact consequence.
23. Check the calm grouper patrols near the final wreck and its optional fact works. Rays, grouper and Lumen should keep moving without blocking the route or shell landings.
24. Stay in the final habitat for about 20 seconds. Expect the one-time “We can stay here a while.” cue. Keep exploring for another minute: no forced restart, blackout, end screen or repeated ending cue. Open/close the menu and revisit another habitat; exploration must remain available. The cue resets on a new app session.

## Sound, comfort and final regressions

25. Listen to breathing, water, distant whale/reef layers and nearby dolphin/fish audio. Check comfortable levels, positional fading, narration clarity and no continuous music or obvious audio cutouts. New Day 6 narration uses offline system-voice placeholders.
26. Walk/lean toward rocks and large-animal bodies, swim against the lateral perimeter and ascend toward the surface. Check collision stops you safely without trapping you. Attempt clear and obstructed teleports; the latter should be rejected. Keep real-world movement inside your safe tracked space.
27. Leave the diver idle in open water for more than 20 seconds. Check the gentle current is subtle; apply movement input and check control returns promptly. Disable current assist in the desktop settings if reviewing that option there.
28. Pause/resume the app and take the headset off/on. Check sound resumes, menus still work, tracking remains correct and no creature/hand state gets stuck.
29. Repeat full head turns in dense reef, wreck, twilight, trench and rays. Note where stutters occur. Subjective smoothness is a wearer check; measured CPU/GPU/frame times and a final full-device performance pass remain outstanding.
