## Smithsonian Open Access — CC0

- Acropora secale: https://3d.si.edu/object/3d/acropora-secale%3Acb2e1f4d-8eaa-454b-b61d-cc78b6b64b7b
- Madrepora hyacinthus: https://www.si.edu/es/object/3d/madrepora-hyacinthus%3A1a0a3b2d-5ef6-4bc0-af39-0a35d95f1f01
- Acropora cervicornis, USNM 74016: https://3d-api.si.edu/content/document/3d_package:dd875177-c986-48e2-9468-8e58f01099d3/document.json

Museum specimen scans converted from GLB to FBX, oriented, normalized, and decimated into three LODs. Colors are artistically tinted to suggest living tissue. The Secale mounting plinth is trimmed from the mesh. The final scene uses Secale instances; the other source scans remain available but are excluded because their mounting blocks are visible. These are artistic environmental assets, not an assertion of scientific habitat accuracy or Smithsonian endorsement.

## Barramundi fish — Microsoft / Khronos, CC0

Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/BarramundiFish

Converted to FBX, normalized, and assigned extracted albedo and normal maps. Used as a visual fish-school asset, not a claim about reef species distribution. Full upstream licensing and metadata attribution are in BarramundiFish-LICENSE.md (asset CC0; associated metadata CC BY 4.0).

## Project-authored assets

Procedural terrain, coral variants, turtle/ray geometry, wrecks, caustic/tissue textures, shaders, movement, and interactions are generated by the included project scripts. Blender conversion scripts and original downloaded source files are retained for reproducibility.


## Loggerhead turtle — Innerscene, CC0

Source and licence: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/loggerhead-sea-turtle-0f7b7b89
Download: https://www.innerscene.com/api/library/loggerhead-sea-turtle-0f7b7b89/download
Library terms: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/terms

Original generalized adult loggerhead study model, released by Innerscene under CC0. Downloaded 2026-10-27. Converted GLB to FBX, extracted albedo and normal textures, rotated into the scene coordinate system, and added underwater materials and procedural flipper animation. It is an authored model, not a photographic scan. The earlier simplified green-turtle geometry has been replaced by this loggerhead.

## Giant manta ray replacement
Innerscene — Giant manta ray, CC0. https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/giant-manta-ray-006f87da . Original generalized study model; not a scan. Converted to FBX, reduced to 16,655 triangles, source textures retained, and animated with a custom wing shader. Original GLB retained in Assets/UnderwaterArt/Scans/GiantManta.glb.
